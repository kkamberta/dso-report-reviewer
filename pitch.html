Architecture & workflowOne compact pipeline from scan export to reviewed report

The workflow is a single provider-neutral section: parse scanner sheets, normalize findings, apply policy rules, validate coverage, and produce reviewer-ready outputs.

Architecture boundary Parsing, decision rules, validation, and report writing are separate layers, so each one can be tested or replaced independently.
Policy-driven decisions Example: a Tomcat CVE on a high-priority asset stays Critical and gets a concrete upgrade control instead of a vague analyst note.
Human review stays focused Analysts review exceptions, severity jumps, missing context, and validation failures instead of rereading every scanner row.
